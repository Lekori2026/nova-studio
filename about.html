Our story

Built on slow dough and strong community.

Founded out of a deep passion for community and the slow art of baking, North Star Bakery opened its doors with a simple goal: to serve as a warm, guiding light for our neighborhood. We believe that good bread takes time, patience, and a dedicated pair of hands. Every loaf we sell is shaped right here on our wooden worktables, continuing traditions passed down through generations.

Our sourcing philosophy

We believe that a great bake starts long before the flour hits the mixing bowl. That is why we partner exclusively with independent regional millers who harvest organic, non-GMO grains. Our dairy, seasonal fruits, and herbs are gathered weekly from local family farms. By honoring the land and the hands that tend it, we ensure that every bite you take supports our local community ecosystem.
